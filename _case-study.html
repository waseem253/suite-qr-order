Suite QR Order — Guest-Facing Hotel Ordering

Scan the room QR → order → pay → routed to the right team, fully backend
suite-qr-order.vercel.app

The problem

An 86-room Maltese hotel wanted guests to scan a QR in their room and order Food & Beverage, Adventure experiences or a Taxi — paid online, premium feel, with each order reaching the right team automatically and the guest never seeing the backend.

The build

A Next.js app: the room number is in the URL (/room/204, guest never types it); three content-driven category pages; F&B orderable 10:00–04:00 Malta time (the window crosses midnight) while the menu stays browseable outside hours; Adventure & Taxi 24/7; real Stripe-hosted Checkout (card data never touches the server); on-screen confirmation; a server-enforced 30-second double-tap block per room; English at launch with Italian + French scaffolded.

Architecture

Room QR ─▶ Next.js ─▶ category pages ─▶ Stripe Checkout └─▶ webhook-authoritative confirmation ├─▶ owner email ├─▶ WhatsApp routed by category (F&B / Adventure / Taxi) └─▶ on-screen confirmation + 30s double-tap block

Stripe, WhatsApp (Meta/Twilio) and the order store sit behind interfaces. With Stripe test keys it is a real card-entry page; without, a clearly-labeled simulated payment runs the identical flow so the demo never breaks. The Stripe webhook is the production-authoritative path — the owner is notified even if the guest closes the tab.

Why it matters: real payments with real edges — midnight-crossing service hours, PCI kept off-server, idempotent notify, server-enforced anti-double-tap, and i18n that's a content drop, not a refactor.
